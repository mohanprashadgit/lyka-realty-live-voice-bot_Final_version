# Task 7 addition: mandatory three-offer presentation

## Implemented

The shared Node voice-agent prompt now proactively introduces free flight ticket booking, Golden Visa assistance and dedicated property purchase support in every suitable new customer sales conversation. It starts after opening permission and basic interest discovery, covers outstanding offers before normal qualification completion, and continues naturally with missing purpose, budget, location and timeline.

Short explanations, natural transitions and mood-sensitive ordering replace a single long advertisement. Questions and interruptions take priority; the agent resumes only the remaining or interrupted explanation. Busy, refusal, distress, end-call and DNC handling override offer completion. Accommodation and transportation assistance remain contextual benefits and do not replace the mandatory three. All prior eligibility, expense and visa-approval safeguards remain.

Three presentation-progress fields use the existing Task 7 notes/interaction metadata path: `flight_offer_status`, `golden_visa_offer_status`, and `purchase_support_offer_status`. Completed explanations are `presented`; explicit acknowledgement can mark them `understood`. Interrupted/invalid values cannot mark completion. Partial updates cannot downgrade understood progress. Presentation alone does not set customer interest or follow-up permission.

Browser and Twilio prompts receive validated saved progress. Returning customers do not receive unnecessary repeats of understood offers. A known customer name alone does not imply offer familiarity. No schema, voice/model, transport, audio, interruption or rejection implementation was replaced.
